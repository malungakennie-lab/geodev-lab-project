## QUESTION

Which catchments in southern part of Malawi are more likely to experience high sedimentation

## WHY IT MATTERS

Sedimentation is a major environmental challenge in Southern Malawi, threatening water security,hydropower generation along the Middle Shire River, and downstream communities through increased flooding. Identifying critically degraded catchment areas will help government and NGOs prioritize targeted restoration, conservation, and water-resource management interventions.
