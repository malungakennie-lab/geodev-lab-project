## Coordinate Reference System (CRS)

The datasets were checked for their original coordinate reference systems (CRS) before processing.

The working CRS selected for the Southern Malawi sedimentation analysis is WGS 84 / UTM Zone 36S (EPSG:32736)

This CRS was selected as the working CRS for spatial analysis because the project involves distance, area, catchment, drainage and raster-based analysis.

## Reprojection

Datasets that were not already in the selected working CRS were reprojected to WGS 84 / UTM Zone 36S (EPSG:32736)

## Reprojected datasets

\- Malawi administrative boundary

\- HydroBASINS catchments

\- HydroRIVERS drainage network

\- Digital Elevation Model (DEM)

\- Soil data

\- Rainfall data

\- Landsat 2020

\- Landsat 2025

## Reason for reprojection

To place the spatial datasets in a common working coordinate system so that they can be compared and used together during spatial analysis.

## Clipping to the Study Area

The datasets were clipped to the Southern Region of Malawi using the Southern Malawi study-area boundary.

## Clipped datasets

\- HydroBASINS

\- DEM

\- Soil data

\- Rainfall data

\- Landsat 2020

\- Landsat 2025

## Reason for clipping

To restrict the datasets to the study area, reduce unnecessary data, and prepare the datasets for the Southern Malawi sedimentation analysis.
